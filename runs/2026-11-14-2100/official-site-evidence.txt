If something is not covered, send us a quick WhatsApp — we usually reply within minutes. How early should I book catering for my event? For weddings and large events (200+ guests), we recommend 2–4 weeks of lead time. Smaller corporate lunches, birthdays, and pujas can usually be booked 3–7 days in advance. Same-week bookings are possible based on availability — message us and we will confirm quickly. Is there a minimum order quantity? No minimum order — we cater intimate gatherings of 10 guests as well as weddings of 2,000. Pricing is per guest, with clear inclusions, so smaller events are still easy to plan and budget. Do you cater pure-veg, jain, or satvik menus? Yes — pure-veg, jain (no onion/garlic), and satvik options are available across all our menus. We also handle mixed dietary preferences (vegan, gluten-free, allergies) when shared in advance. What does the price per guest include? Our quoted price typically includes the menu items listed, fresh preparation, hot delivery, serving staff, basic equipment, set-up, and clean-up. Live counters, premium crockery, decor, or custom requests are itemised separately so there are no surprises. Do you handle delivery and serving staff? Yes. We arrange hot, on-time delivery across Bangalore and provide trained, well-presented serving staff with every event. Our team handles set-up, service through the event, and clean-up after the last guest leaves. How do payments and confirmations work? We share a written quote with the menu and inclusions. A small advance confirms the booking; the balance is settled before or on the day of the event. Cash, UPI, and bank transfer are all accepted. Have an event in mind?
