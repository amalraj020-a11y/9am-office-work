- Official website: https://handymanservice.in/
- Public business email: info@handymanservice.in
- The official site lists home cleaning, plumbing, electrical, carpentry, appliance repair, care, vehicle, taxi, and driver services.
- Its published process asks customers for their need, location, preferred time, and work details, after which the team reviews requirements, availability, and a suitable professional.
- The booking form publicly shows name, mobile number, email, service, city/area, and booking details.

## Hypothesis

Because the site covers many service categories and says the team reviews each requirement and availability, incoming bookings may require repetitive manual checking for service type, urgency, location, preferred time, and missing information. This is an operational hypothesis, not a claim supplied by the business.
